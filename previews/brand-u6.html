Lockups

Four arrangements. Horizontal is the default; use the others only when space demands it.

Construction

Everything is proportional to the name size (N), so the lockup scales as one piece.

ElementRule
Name "resale"Inter 700, letter-spacing −2%, Teal 800 #024E53. Size = N.
Domain ".com.pk"Inter 700 at 0.7 N, Slate 500 #64748B, sharing the name's baseline. The full stop is a normal full stop in the domain colour.
UnderlineUnder "com.pk" only. Thickness 0.14 × domain size (≈ 0.1 N), never below 2px. Gap below baseline equal to its thickness. Both ends cut at 30° from vertical, matching the symbol's hexagon. Orange 600 #FD7009.
FoldAt the right end, a small tab folds down beneath the stroke in Orange 700 #E55106, echoing the symbol's ribbon folds. Width 2.2 × stroke, depth 2.6 × stroke.
SymbolHeight 1.55 N, vertically centred on the name. Gap between symbol and name 0.42 N.
Clear spaceAt least the symbol's height × 0.25 on every side (guidelines §10: 12.5% of the logo width, rounded up for the wider lockup).

Backgrounds

Full colour on white and light neutrals. On dark: white name and white symbol, orange stroke kept. On teal: same, with Teal Soft 2 for the domain. For one-colour printing, everything goes ink or white.

Website

resale.com.pk header, scrolled header, dark mode, footer, browser tab and link preview.

Mobile app & mobile web

Home-screen icon, splash, app bar in light and dark, and a push notification. The app icon is the symbol alone, never the wordmark.

Social media

Profile avatars use the symbol only (circle crops cut off text). Covers, posts and stories carry the full lockup.

Print & shop

Business card, invoice header, verified-shop window sticker and storefront sign.
